## Ready-to-use credit

[kagan.in](https://kagan.in)

```markdown
[kagan.in](https://kagan.in)
```

In an app, add a “kagan.in” link opening `https://kagan.in` in your About, Credits, or Acknowledgements screen. The website link alone is sufficient; no separate personal name or social profile is required. The credit should be readable and reachable through the normal interface. It does not need to be on the main screen.

| Use | Where the credit goes |
| --- | --- |
| Shipped app, paid app, client work, or website | Normally accessible About/Credits/Acknowledgements screen or page |
| Source-only fork or reusable package | README or primary documentation; retain LICENSE |
| Public standalone video or demo | Its caption, description, or credits |
| Private local learning or internal experimentation | No public-facing credit required |

A tweet is welcome, but it does not replace the credit in an app. A credit buried only in source files does not satisfy the app requirement. Someone using your fork in a product inherits the attribution obligation for the incorporated material.

## Want to omit the visible credit?

Contact [@kaganin on X](https://x.com/kaganin) before distributing or publicly making the work available without credit. Include:

- Your product name and URL, if available.
- Which experiment or code you used.
- Where it will be distributed and which credit exception you want.

Wait for an explicit written permission. Asking is not approval. Permission only covers the agreed product and scope, and does not waive third-party asset licenses.
